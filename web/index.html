Display and sensor

Wi-Fi networks

The monitor joins the strongest saved network in range, and works without Wi-Fi elsewhere.

    Pick an address outside the range your router hands out. If the monitor can't be reached afterwards, its Wi-Fi page still shows the address; Forget Wi-Fi in its settings removes it.

    To set up Wi-Fi from a phone instead: on the monitor, hold D1 for the settings and choose Set up Wi-Fi.

    Home Assistant

    Sends the readings every 30 s to Home Assistant through an MQTT broker, such as its Mosquitto add-on. The monitor then appears in Home Assistant by itself, as a device with a sensor for each value.

    Maintenance
    Clear the sensor's errors

    The sensor keeps reporting an error until it's cleared, even once it's gone. If it comes back, it's still there.

    Clean the fan

    Full speed for 10 s to blow out dust; readings pause for about 40 s.

    Heat the humidity sensor

    After a long time in very humid air, the humidity reads too high; a second of heat fixes it. Readings pause for about a minute.

    Calibrate CO₂

    Only after at least 3 minutes in fresh outdoor air, with the reference value of that air.

    ppm
    Reset the CO₂ calibration

    Erases every CO₂ calibration, e.g. to undo one made with the wrong reference. Self-calibration starts over.

    Restart

    Firmware update

    Upload a firmware.bin (not firmware.factory.bin), then confirm it on the monitor by holding any of its buttons for 5 s. If the new firmware can't rejoin Wi-Fi within 5 minutes, the monitor goes back to the current one.
